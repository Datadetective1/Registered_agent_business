## Planned sections

- A one-sentence definition, and other names for the role (statutory agent, resident agent, agent for service of process)
- What the agent receives: service of process (lawsuits, subpoenas) and official state notices
- Why states require one: a reliable in-state address where the business can always be reached
- Who can serve: an individual resident or a registered agent company, with a physical street address (no P.O. boxes)
- What the registered agent address is not for: banking, marketplace accounts, insurance, Google Business Profile verification, everyday mail
- What happens if papers are missed: default judgments and loss of good standing (cite sources)
- How a registered agent service delivers documents (scan, upload, email alert)
