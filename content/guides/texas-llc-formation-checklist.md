## Planned sections

- Before you file
  - A name that's distinguishable on Texas records, with an allowed ending (LLC, L.L.C., Limited Liability Company…)
  - A registered agent and registered office with a Texas street address, and the agent's consent
  - Member-managed or manager-managed, and the names and addresses of the governing persons
  - An organizer, and an effective date (on filing or a delayed date)
- Filing: Certificate of Formation (Form 205) and the state filing fee (pulled from current settings, verify against the SOS fee schedule)
- After approval
  - Get an EIN from the IRS (free)
  - Write a company agreement (not filed with the state)
  - Franchise tax and Public Information Report: first report due date and the no-tax-due threshold (verify current figures)
  - Local licenses and permits, and a business bank account
- Printable checklist summary
