Every LLC and corporation must name a **registered agent**: someone who accepts legal papers and official state notices for the business. Many new owners wonder whether they can simply do it themselves. In most states, including Texas, the answer is yes, as long as you meet the requirements.

## What the role requires

A registered agent generally must:

- have a **physical street address in the state** (a P.O. box doesn't qualify), which is listed publicly as the business's registered office;
- be **available at that address during normal business hours** to accept service of process in person; and
- reliably **forward documents** to the right people in the business and keep the state informed if the address changes.

An individual who lives in the state, or a business entity authorized to act as an agent there, can usually serve.

## Reasons owners serve as their own agent

- **Cost.** There's no annual fee.
- **Control.** You receive documents directly.

## Reasons owners use a service instead

- **Privacy.** The registered office address is part of the public record. If you're your own agent, that is often your home address.
- **Availability.** You must be at the address during business hours. Travel, remote work, or a home office can make that hard.
- **Discretion.** Being served with a lawsuit at your workplace or in front of customers can be uncomfortable.
- **Organization.** A service records each document and keeps a digital copy in one place.
- **Multiple states.** Businesses registered in more than one state need an agent in each.

## If you change your mind later

You can change your registered agent at any time by filing a statement of change with the state. In Texas, that's done with the Secretary of State, and a filing fee applies.

## The bottom line

Serving as your own registered agent is allowed in most states and can work well if you're consistently available at an in-state address you're comfortable making public. If you value privacy or flexibility, a registered agent service is a modest annual cost.
