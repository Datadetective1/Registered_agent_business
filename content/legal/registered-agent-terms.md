## What the service includes

For each business covered by an active subscription, we (directly or through our commercial registered agent partner):

- maintain a registered office address in the covered state during normal business hours;
- accept service of process and official correspondence from the Secretary of State and other government agencies addressed to your business;
- scan those documents and make them available in your online account; and
- notify the contact on file by email when a new document is available.

## Agent of record

Registered agent service is provided in partnership with a nationwide commercial registered agent. Depending on the state, our partner may be the registered agent of record. Your account shows the exact name and address to list with the state.

## Intended use of the address

The registered agent address may be used only as your business's registered office. It is not a mailing address for general business mail, packages, bank or vendor correspondence, or a principal office. You agree to keep your contact information current, check your account and email regularly, review documents promptly, and take any action they require. Many legal documents have strict deadlines. We don't evaluate, respond to, or give advice about documents we receive. If you're served with a lawsuit, consult a licensed attorney promptly.

For an existing business, you're responsible for filing any statement of change required to appoint us (or our partner) as your registered agent, unless you've purchased a service that includes that filing.
