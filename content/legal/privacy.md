This Privacy Policy explains how {{brand.legalName}} ("{{brand.name}}," "we," "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use {{appUrl}} and our services.

## Information we collect

**Information you provide.** Your name, email address, phone number, and password when you create an account. Business information you provide for orders, such as your company's name, addresses, and the names and addresses of its members, managers, organizer, and contact person. Messages you send us, and documents you upload.

**Documents received for you.** As your registered agent, we receive legal and government documents addressed to your business and store copies in your account.

**Payment information.** Payments are processed by Stripe. We receive limited information such as the payment amount, status, and the last four digits and brand of your card. We do not receive or store full card numbers.

**Technical information.** Our servers record limited technical information, such as IP address and browser type, in security and audit logs to protect accounts and prevent fraud.

**Analytics.** We measure aggregate website usage (for example, how many visitors view our pricing page) without advertising cookies and without identifying individual visitors.

## How we use information

We use information to provide and support our services (including preparing filings and forwarding documents); communicate with you about orders, documents, renewals, and your account; process payments; secure our services and prevent fraud; comply with legal obligations; and improve our website.

## How we share information

We share information only as needed to provide our services:

- **Fulfillment partners**, including our commercial registered agent and filing partner, to provide registered agent service and submit filings.
- **Government agencies**, when we submit filings on your behalf. Information in state business filings (such as your company's name, registered agent, and governing persons) generally becomes **public record**.
- **Service providers** who help us operate (payment processing, email delivery, hosting), under obligations to protect the information.
- **Legal requirements**, when required by law or to protect rights and safety.
