## We are a filing service, not a law firm

We don't provide legal, tax, or accounting advice. We don't advise on which type of entity is right for you, how to structure ownership or management, or the tax consequences of your choices. No attorney–client relationship is formed.

## Your information

Your filing is prepared from your answers. You're responsible for the accuracy and completeness of the information you provide, including names, addresses, and the management structure of your company. Review everything carefully before submitting.

## Government decisions

The Secretary of State decides whether to accept a filing, including whether a business name is available. We can't guarantee approval of a name or filing, or how long the state will take.

## After formation

Forming an entity is only the first step. Depending on your business, you may also need an EIN, an operating agreement, local licenses or permits, and to file ongoing reports such as Texas franchise tax reports. These are your responsibility unless you purchase a service that expressly includes them.
